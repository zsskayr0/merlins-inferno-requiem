# Merlin's Inferno: Requiem

> Survive the inferno. Conquer the unknown.

A Minecraft mod for **NeoForge 1.21.1** built around three lineages of equipment, each with its own origin, creatures and playstyle. Currently in early alpha (`0.0.12-alpha`), so expect rough edges.

## The Three Roads

| Road | Origin | Gear | In a nutshell |
|------|--------|------|---------------|
| **Druidic** | Overworld – the Hallowed Grove | Rowanwood | Sturdy and wise. Mines like Diamond, hits like Iron, with more durability than Diamond. +30% damage to magical creatures, −30% to plain animals. |
| **Infernal** | The Nether | Demonblood | On par with Netherite, with better enchantability. Hits humans (villagers, illagers, witches) for 10% more. The armor's *Demon's Fury* halves fire damage and cuts hostile mobs' damage by a quarter, but neutral mobs hurt 25% more. |
| **Angelical** | Lyrium geodes in the mountains | Seraphium | The strongest of the three, and the hardest to earn. Every blow Sanctifies its target and is meant for evil: extra damage to demons and hostiles, much less to neutral and peaceful mobs. |

## Progression: the Circles
You start in the **First Circle**. Each road has a Circle 1 boss who drops a key item: **Ostara** (Eve's Secret), **Andras** (Book of Contracts) and **Elias** (Flame of God). Lay the three keys around a Nether Star in **Pandora's Box**, found in the Sacred Church, to perform the ritual that opens the **Second Circle** (it costs your life outside Hardcore). Circle 2 brings doubled essence drops, monster leather, Starved in the Nether, hostile cultists, minable Corrupted Obsidian and the Oblivion and Forest keys, forged in the opened Box.

## What You'll Find

### The Hallowed Grove
A new temperate forest biome with two trees: **Ashwood**, a common building wood (full set: planks, stairs, slabs, doors, signs and more), and the rare, towering **Rowanwood**.
Look for a **Druid's Sanctuary** - a clearing ringed by wooden pillars, with a hearth, a chest and a Druid on watch.

- **The Druid** - a peaceful guardian. He hunts the undead and flees from the Dullahan. Trade 20 emeralds for a *Mundane Essence*, the catalyst for Rowanwood gear.
- **The Dullahan** - a headless knight, one with his black steed, who stalks the Grove at night. Tough and dangerous; gold paralyzes him for a moment. He drops *Fae Essence*.
- **Ostara, the Spring Deity** - the Druidic Circle 1 boss. She descends on a Rowanwood at dawn every third day and fades at dusk, flinging petals and healing the monsters around her.
- **Druid's Touch** - a rare enchantment. Without it, Rowanwood logs give plain Ashwood.
- **Edenweed** - a bush that grows around Rowanwood trunks. Dry it on a **Drying Rack**, boil it into a **Minor Sight Potion** and the *Druidic Trance* shows you ores, chests and every living thing through walls - at the price of a couch-lock.

### The Nether
- **Imps** - small flying demons that love gold. Alone they flee; in packs of three or more they dive at you and steal your gold. They drop *Demon Blood*.
- **Grymns** - the Nether's Circle 1 miniboss: a low-flying phantom that lurks and goes for the throat.
- **Andras, the Forgotten Demon** - the Infernal Circle 1 boss, guarding the Ancient Battlefield. He calls Imps and brands you with the *Mark of Debt*.
- **The Starved** - a relentless, ever-hungrier beast. Appears only from Circle 2.
- **The Hell Forge** - a large multiblock forge that burns Lava Buckets or Blaze Powder, cooks faster than a furnace, and is the only place Demonblood Scrap becomes a bar.
- **Enchantments** - *Bane of Humanity* and *Evil*.

### The Sacred Church & Lyrium
- **Lyrium geodes** hide in the mountains, blazing with light. Undead and demons nearby are set ablaze with the Sanctified curse. Lyrium also appears as scattered ore.
- Lyrium is refined in stages (Raw -> Impure -> Refined...). Refined Lyrium can be traded from master-level Clerics or the Sacred Priest, or found in pillager loot.
- **Sanctified** - the touch of Raw Lyrium. It burns like poison and **can kill you**, but bearers hit undead harder and spread the effect to what they strike.
- **The Sacred Church** - a ruined chapel with a congregation of neutral cultists, guarded by the **Sacred Priest**. Once he falls, a diamond on the **Celestial Altar** wakes **Elias**, the Angelical Circle 1 boss, whose Great Bell tolls to strengthen him until it shatters in fury. Elias drops *Celestial Essence*, the catalyst for Seraphium.

### The Legendary Swords
Any boss or miniboss may, very rarely, drop a **Hollow Relic** - a Diamond-strong sword with one use, the core of the legendary swords. Put it in the centre of the crafting grid with four materials: **Eden's Thorn** (Druidic, a poisoning dagger), **Debt Collector** (Infernal, an axe that sets targets ablaze, with Evil I built in and bonus damage to anything weaker than you) and **Vigil's Sorrow** (Angelical, Sanctified II on every hit, Holy I built in, 1.5x damage to anything stronger than you). The **Hell Seal** needed for the Debt Collector drops from Grymns and Andras, or comes nine at a time from a Book of Contracts. Mythic weapons will follow in the Second Circle.

### Ebony and the Dark Coven (Circle 2)
**Ebony Debris** hides in Oblivion (and, very rarely, in Ancient Debris); its scrap, as well as the drops of Oblivion's bosses, is smelted into **Ebony Bars** in the Hell Forge. Ebony tools and armor sit above Seraphium, and the full armor set grants Fire Resistance IV.

- **Cain, the First Immortal** - the Circle 2 Infernal boss. He carries the last form of the Dark Coven and withers whatever he strikes; once he is half dead he invokes his own pact.
- **The Flesh Golem** - the Circle 2 Infernal miniboss: a towering horned mass that heals with every blow.
- **The Dark Coven** - a legendary sword in seven stages. Right-click to invoke the coven (Strength, Speed and Night Vision, paid for with the Wither); each stage is a smithing step with a Withered Bone and an Ebony Bar, and the last one asks for a Nether Star and seals the Demonic Pact. The blade lights up with more runes at every stage.

### Other
- **The Champion Seeker** - a compass that points at the nearest boss or miniboss.
- **Benches**, **Ornated Carpets**, Flesh blocks and more decoration.
- Optional compat: bosses leave a [Lootr](https://modrinth.com/mod/lootr) chest with per-player loot when it is installed.

### In-game Guide
With [Patchouli](https://modrinth.com/mod/patchouli) installed you get a guide book covering everything above, plus advancements for each armor set.

## Installation

1. Install **Minecraft 1.21.1** with **NeoForge 21.1.x**.
2. Download **[GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib)** 4.8+ (required).
3. *(Optional)* Download **Patchouli** for the in-game guide.
4. Drop the mod's `.jar` into your `mods` folder and launch.

Available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
